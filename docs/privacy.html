Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Murmur is a desktop app that transcribes audio and video on your own computer. It was built so that your recordings never have to leave your machine. This policy explains what that means in practice.

The short version

  • Your audio, video and transcripts are processed and stored only on your computer.
  • Murmur has no account, no analytics, no telemetry and no ads.
  • We never receive your recordings or transcripts.

What stays on your computer

Murmur stores the following in your user data folder (on Windows, %APPDATA%\Murmur). Settings has a button that opens this folder.

  • Transcripts and summaries you create, in the app's library.
  • Recordings you make with the microphone. These are temporary files, deleted as soon as they are transcribed (or within a day if a transcription fails). The transcript is kept in your library.
  • Speech models you download.
  • Settings, such as your preferred model and last export folder.
  • Your license key, which contains the name, email and plan you bought with.

You can delete any of this at any time by deleting items in the app or removing the folder. Uninstalling Murmur does not remove it automatically, so your work survives reinstalls.

When Murmur uses the internet

Transcription, summaries, recording and license checks all work with no network connection. Murmur only connects to the internet when you click Download in Setup to fetch a speech model. That download comes from Hugging Face (huggingface.co and its content delivery network), which will see your IP address and a standard download request, as with any website. No audio or transcript data is sent. Hugging Face's own privacy policy applies to that request.

Microphone

Murmur asks for microphone access only so you can record a clip to transcribe. All other browser-style permissions are denied inside the app. Recordings are processed locally like any other file.

Buying a license

Purchases are handled by our payment provider, which acts as the merchant of record. It collects your name, email, billing details and payment information to process the order, calculate tax and prevent fraud, under its own privacy policy. We receive your name, email, the plan you bought and order details so we can issue your license key and help with support or refunds. We do not receive your full card number.

Support emails

If you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you. Please don't send recordings or transcripts unless you choose to share them to solve a problem.

This website

This site uses no cookies, analytics, trackers, web fonts or third-party scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log basic request data, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Murmur is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you control it directly. For the small amount of purchase information we hold, you can ask us to access, correct or delete it by emailing us. We'll keep only what we are legally required to keep, such as records needed for tax.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If a change affects what leaves your computer, we'll say so clearly in the release notes.
