What it does

  • Opens mp3, m4a, wav, mp4, mov, mkv and other audio or video files.
  • Records straight from your microphone.
  • Queues a batch of files or a whole folder.
  • Writes a short summary of each transcript.
  • Keeps a searchable library on your disk. Delete anything, any time.
  • Exports to txt, md or srt.

How it works

  1. Install Murmur and download a speech model once from the Setup screen.
  2. Add a file or start a recording.
  3. Read, search or export the transcript.

Questions

Does my audio get uploaded?

No. Transcription runs on your computer and your files stay on your disk.

Does Murmur use the internet at all?

Only when you click Download in Setup to fetch a speech model from Hugging Face. After that it works offline.

Where is my data kept?

In your user data folder. Settings has a button that opens it.
